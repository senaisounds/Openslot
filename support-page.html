OpenSlot Support

Need Help?

We're here to help you with any questions about OpenSlot. Whether you need assistance with creating events, managing your profile, or have technical issues, our support team is ready to assist you.

How to Get Support

Email Support: Send us an email and we'll respond within 24 hours.

In-App Support: Use the "Contact Support" button in your OpenSlot app for quick assistance.

Common Questions

How do I create an event? Tap the "+" button on the home screen and follow the setup process.

How do I join an event? Browse events and tap "Join" on any event you're interested in.

How do I update my profile? Go to your profile page and tap "Edit Profile".
