🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us

Last updated: January 2024

Overview

OpenSlot ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service").

Quick Summary: We collect minimal information needed to provide our service. We never sell your personal data, and we use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and password when you create an account
  • Profile Information: Display name, bio, performer type (musician, poet, DJ, comedian), and optional profile photo
  • Event Interactions: Events you bookmark, sign up for, or review
  • User Content: Reviews, comments, and ratings you submit

Information Automatically Collected

  • Location Data: Approximate location to show nearby events (only when you grant permission)
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version for troubleshooting
  • Usage Analytics: App usage patterns to improve our service (anonymized)
  • Technical Data: IP address, crash reports, performance data

How We Use Your Information

Primary Uses

  • Provide and maintain the OpenSlot service
  • Show you relevant open mic events and opportunities near you
  • Enable communication between performers and venues
  • Send important updates about events you've signed up for
  • Improve app functionality and user experience

Communications

  • Event notifications and reminders
  • Important service updates and security alerts
  • Optional marketing communications (you can opt-out anytime)
  • Responses to your support requests

Information Sharing

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

When We May Share Information

  • With Venues: Your performer profile when you sign up for events (name, performer type, contact info)
  • Public Information: Reviews and ratings you choose to make public
  • Service Providers: Trusted partners who help us operate the app (analytics, hosting, customer support)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our users' safety
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger or acquisition (with notice to users)

Your Privacy Rights

You Can

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Update: Modify your account and profile information anytime
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Control Location: Turn location services on/off in your device settings
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to personal data by our team
  • Secure hosting infrastructure
  • Regular data backups with encryption

Note: While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Event History: Kept to provide performance tracking features
  • Analytics Data: Anonymized data retained for service improvement
  • Deleted Accounts: Most data deleted within 30 days, some anonymized data may be retained for analytics

Children's Privacy

OpenSlot is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

International Users

If you are using OpenSlot outside the United States, please note that we may transfer your information to and process it in the United States, where our servers are located. By using our service, you consent to this transfer.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
  • Sending an email notification
  • Displaying a prominent notice in the app

Your continued use of OpenSlot after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
