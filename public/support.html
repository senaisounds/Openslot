Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Download the OpenSlot app and tap "Sign Up" to create your account. You can sign up with your email address or use social media accounts like Google or Facebook.

How do I find events near me?

Open the app and allow location access when prompted. The app will automatically show you events happening in your area. You can also search for specific types of events or browse by category.

How do I reserve a spot at an event?

Tap on any event to view its details, then tap "Reserve Spot" to book your place. You'll receive a confirmation and can view your reservations in your profile.

Can I host my own events?

Yes! If you're a venue owner or event organizer, you can create events through our host portal. Contact us for more information about becoming a host.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings in the app, then tap "Delete Account" to permanently remove your account and all associated data. This action cannot be undone.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major credit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay for event reservations and payments.

How do I get a refund?

Refund policies vary by event. Contact the event organizer directly or reach out to our support team for assistance with refund requests.

Common Issues & Solutions

App won't load events

  • Check your internet connection
  • Make sure location services are enabled
  • Try closing and reopening the app
  • Update to the latest version of the app

Can't sign in to my account

  • Verify your email and password are correct
  • Try the "Forgot Password" option
  • Check if your account has been locked
  • Contact support if the issue persists

Payment not going through

  • Verify your payment method is valid
  • Check if your card has sufficient funds
  • Try a different payment method
  • Contact your bank if the issue continues

Privacy & Data

How do I access my data?

You can view and download your data through the app settings. Go to Settings > Privacy > Data Export to request a copy of your information.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account, go to Settings in the app and tap "Delete Account". This will permanently remove all your data including events, reservations, and profile information.

Is my data secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your personal information. We never sell your data to third parties.
