❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find open mics near me?
Open the OpenSlot app, allow location access, and browse nearby open mic events. You can filter by event type (music, comedy, poetry, DJ) and distance.
How do I sign up for a performance slot?
Tap on any event to view details, then tap "Reserve Slot" or "Sign Up". Follow the venue's specific sign-up process and requirements.
Can venues add their events to OpenSlot?
Yes! Venues can create accounts and list their open mic nights, open deck sessions, and other performance opportunities. Contact us at business@openslot.me for details.
Is OpenSlot free to use?
Yes, OpenSlot is free for performers to discover and sign up for events. Some premium features may be added in the future.
What types of events are on OpenSlot?
We focus on open participation events: open mic nights for musicians and comedians, open deck sessions for DJs, poetry readings, jam sessions, and other performance opportunities.
My event isn't showing up or has wrong information
Contact us at support@openslot.me with the event details and we'll help correct or add the information.
How do I report inappropriate content or behavior?
Send us details at support@openslot.me. We take community safety seriously and will investigate all reports promptly.

🔧 Technical Issues

If you're experiencing technical problems:

  • Make sure you have the latest version of the app
  • Check your internet connection
  • Restart the app
  • If problems persist, email us at support@openslot.me with your device model and OS version
