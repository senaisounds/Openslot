## 💡 Design Principles Applied

### 1. Respect User Time
- ❌ Force users to read everything
- ✅ Give essential info, let them explore

### 2. Progressive Disclosure
- ❌ Teach everything upfront
- ✅ Show what matters now, teach rest later

### 3. Reduce Friction
- ❌ Many steps before value
- ✅ Quick path to using app

### 4. Build Trust Early
- ❌ "Read all this before you start"
- ✅ "Welcome! Let's go!"
