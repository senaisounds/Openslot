❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an event?

To create an event, tap the "+" button in the bottom navigation bar. Fill in your event details including title, description, date, time, and location. You can also add photos and set ticket prices.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account, open the app and go to Profile → Profile Actions → Delete Account. You'll be asked to confirm your decision. Note that this action cannot be undone.

How do I get paid for my events?

To receive payouts, go to Profile → Payout Settings and add your payment method. You can choose from bank account, PayPal, Venmo, or Cash App. Payouts are processed after your event ends.

Can I edit my event after creating it?

Yes! Go to My Events, find your event, and tap the edit icon. You can modify most details as long as the event hasn't started yet.

How do I save events I'm interested in?

When browsing events, tap the bookmark icon to save an event. You can view all your saved events by going to Profile → Saved Events.

What if I need to cancel my event?

Go to My Events, find your event, and tap the delete icon. You'll be asked to confirm the cancellation. Refunds will be processed automatically for any tickets sold.
