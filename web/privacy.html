Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

OpenSlot ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you:

  • Create an account
  • Create or attend events
  • Add payment information
  • Contact our support team

Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile information.

Event Information: Event details, photos, location data, and attendance records.

Payment Information: Payment method details (processed securely through our payment partners).

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Process payments and transactions
  • Send you important updates and notifications
  • Improve our app and user experience
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • We use secure cloud infrastructure (Firebase)
  • Access to your data is limited to authorized personnel
  • We regularly review and update our security practices

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Update your information: Modify your account details through the app
  • Delete your account: Remove your account and data completely
  • Opt out of communications: Unsubscribe from marketing emails

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time by:

  1. Opening the OpenSlot app
  2. Going to Profile → Profile Actions → Delete Account
  3. Confirming your decision

Note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently removed.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services:

  • Firebase: For app functionality and data storage
  • Payment Processors: For secure payment processing
  • Analytics: To improve our app (anonymized data only)

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@openslot.me
  • Support: https://openslot.me/support.html
